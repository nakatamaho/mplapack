# Measurement and implementation notes

## Scope and arithmetic

This is the goal10 **item4b** supplement, after item5. Baseline is `7e0b1f55c`.
The previous items1–5 report is historical evidence, not overwritten by this
supplement. The final source identity and artifact hashes are in `provenance.json`.

Upper remains **left-looking**. There is no right-looking conversion, transpose
buffer, reciprocal substitution, reduction-tree reassociation, default-dispatch
change, CPU fast-path change or external MPC-CUDA edit.

- BF transpose HERK assigns independent outputs to tiles, retaining increasing-k
  arithmetic, real diagonal treatment, zero cases and final alpha/beta. Small
  dd/td panels use8-wide tiles; qd retains its more conservative tile choice.
- Left/Upper/transpose TRSM panels retain private alpha*B, increasing-k
  subtraction, pivot broadcasts and actual division. The cooperative geometry
  is64×2 threads; it does not change the reference dot-product order.
- Small BF resident transpose GEMM uses8-wide tiles where selected.
- MPFR fixed-complex C/N GEMM and Upper HERK use8×8 value-only tiles for the
  supported value sizes (through1024-bit complex values). Larger fixed values
  and runtime-complex updates retain their bounded generic jobs.
- Runtime-real Upper TRSM uses private matrix accumulators for m8..64 and the
  existing8192-thread pool with128-column chunks.
- MPFR complex solves retain all arena-safe joins and bounds. The final Upper
  schedule releases the far stream after the panel, queues **both independent
  prefix GEMMs before either solve**, and then executes the solves. Prefix input
  rows are disjoint from current output rows; the two output column ranges do
  not overlap. This restores GEMM stream overlap without changing arithmetic,
  arena allocation/indexing policy or failure publication.

All factor outputs, `info`, pivots, signed zeros, padding and failure cases remain
subject to exact comparisons. The existing public fixed-complex BLAS zero-sign
allowance is explicitly separate; see [validation](validation/README.md).

## Timing protocol

GPU: NVIDIA A10080GB **PCIe**,108 SMs. The nominal1410MHz,65536 registers/SM,
167936 shared bytes/SM and64 warps/SM come from the recorded nsys device database.
They are device properties, not sampled achieved clocks or occupancy.

- Explicit CUDA factor entry, no CPU fallback; one warmup, best of three.
  Whole-entry time includes the entry's transfers/setup, unlike resident kernels.
- Same immutable factor executable per backend on both sides; only the selected
  frozen library changes. MPFR actual requested precision is set correctly.
- Required matrix:84 cases per stage. dd/td/qd and MPFR512, real/complex,
 1024/2048/4096, LU/Lower/Upper; optional MPFR256/1024 at1024.
- Repeated controls:18 cases, three alternating rounds, each warmup1/best3;
  publish the median of those three samples. Runtime200 supplement: six cases
  per stage at512, including LU and Lower controls.
- Public-kernel sweep:84 points per stage, one warmup/best3 and `--long 999999`.
  BF binaries embed kernels, so candidate BF binaries—not merely a changed
  library path—are required. MPFR public Cgemm is an unchanged control, **not**
  evidence for the changed resident complex updates.
- Resident complex512 GEMM/HERK: six timing points per stage and eight exact
  boundary-dump pairs. Both stages are rebuilt and interleaved.
- Empty GPU-process receipts bracket accepted timings and profiles. Numerical
  tests may share the GPU with one another, but not with accepted measurements.
  Incomplete/nonempty attempts do not enter accepted data.

The initial fully tested/timed candidate exposed serialized MPFR complex streams
in its final profiles. Its complete data and source snapshots are preserved
locally in `upper-pre-overlap/`; they are superseded schedules, not discarded
outliers. After the host launch correction, the whole-factor pairs and resident
sweep were repeated, candidate public kernels and14 profiles rerun. Frozen
baseline public-kernel timings and six baseline profiles remain unchanged.

## Roofline denominators

Both models are generated by the repository's `benchmark/roofline.py`:

- **Revised:** [`peaks.csv`](../../roofline/peaks.csv), MPFR full-width random
  significands and GEMM sign/exponent-gap mix for madd/add/mul. Division retains
  the original primitive calibration.
- **Historical:** [`peaks-double-operands-20261104.csv`](../../roofline/peaks-double-operands-20261104.csv),
  the earlier double-derived operands/same-sign additions. It overestimates
  MPFR arithmetic throughput relative to the revised calibration.

The comparison uses `100*t_bound/t_measured`, with
`t_bound=max(t_arithmetic,t_memory)`. Only the denominator changes between these
models; measured seconds do not. Counts/calibration are models, not hardware
counters; small excursions above100% are not measured efficiencies above a
physical limit. Native BF peaks are unchanged. Runtime200 has no calibrated
arithmetic peak here and is not assigned an invented percentage.

Tables cover84 public-kernel points, six resident points and the historical56
item5 GEMM points per stage. Recomputing item5 percentages is **not** a new item5
timing campaign. No invented GEMM-plateau values are supplied by an empty sweep.

## Profiles, geometry and limitations

Profiles contain four calls (warmup plus three measurements), not just the best
call. Group seconds and percentages are sums of kernel durations. Concurrent
kernels overlap, so their sum can exceed whole-entry wall time. The timeline CSV
also gives the union of kernel intervals and sum/union: a value above1 proves
interval overlap, **not** occupancy or speedup by itself. First-to-last-kernel
span includes gaps and is not an entry timer.

Grid/block X/Y, register metadata, shared memory and launches/duration with fewer
than108 blocks are recorded. These are launch facts, not measured active SMs.
Generic MPFR far jobs may launch skipped prefix blocks to select their existing
arena pool, so raw grid counts can exceed useful-block counts. Triangular tiles
also have inactive outputs/blocks. `LOCAL0` does not imply a zero call stack.

`ncu` failed with `ERR_NVGPUCTRPERM`. No measured occupancy, register-stall,
shared-bank-conflict or stall-counter claim is made. Full MPFR-complex memcheck
remains **UNVERIFIED** under the approved exception; two value-only helper
memchecks do not lift it. A denied debugger attach was not worked around.

A real-SYRK tiling pilot regressed (~.487→.664s for four1024 profiles) and was
reverted. A128-register/eight-block probe could not compile: callees required
254 registers (`mp_cmul_re/im`) and180 (`factor_real_op`). Both attempted builds
returned255; an idle queued probe was cancelled143. There are **no accepted
bounded-register timings**. A `cuobjdump` directory-input error in that probe was
harness misuse, not evidence of exhausted GPU/host memory. No register/stack
policy workaround was adopted.
